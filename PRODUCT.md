## Users
- **Primary:** Ishita Rao, 15, Class 10 (CBSE), Pune. An NSEJS/IJSO Olympiad aspirant who is bored by routine drill and wants hard, arguable questions. She studies alone in 25-minute evening slots (8–9 PM), on her Android phone (Chrome, 360 px) or the family laptop. Her job here: find out, through evidence, where a tree's mass really comes from.
- **Secondary:** Mr. D'Souza, her Olympiad coach. He reviews every AI judgement, then approves it or overrides it with his own text.

## Product Purpose
The Willow Mystery puts one question on trial: where did the 74.4 kg a willow gained come from? The learner predicts, weighs van Helmont's ledger (the soil lost 0.057 kg, which is 0.08% of the gain), and explains her idea to three AI agents: Dr. Leaf (Explainer), Skeptic (Challenger) and the Evidence Judge. She then settles their disagreement herself with a drying-oven tool (fresh vs dry mass) and completes an adaptive challenge set. Success: she can explain that most of a plant's dry mass comes from CO₂ in the air, and she loses the soil and water misconceptions.

## Positioning
The disagreement between the agents is real, and the learner resolves it with evidence; the app never names the winner. A human teacher has the final word over every AI judgement.
